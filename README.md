# Hi, I'm Jama Abdi Jama 👋

I build **[NGOsoftware](https://ngosoftware.app)** – a portal for NGOs to run their programmes, donors and finances in one place.

Any NGO can have its own portal on NGOsoftware, with its own name, logo, users and reports.

### What NGOsoftware does

- **Activity registers** – one register per donor; record what was done each day, with photos, PDFs or Drive links as evidence
- **Donor accounts and statements** – activities and costs are billed to the donor, payments are set against them, statements in English or Turkish
- **Invoices, receipts and letters** – numbered documents with letterhead, signature and stamp, as PDF in one click
- **Programmes** – orphan sponsorship, cataract operations, water wells, Qurbani season and beneficiary lists
- **Books** – double-entry books built from the records: trial balance, income and expenditure, balance sheet, funds by donor
- **Staff, payroll and assets** – staff list and ID cards, monthly payroll, asset register with barcode labels

### How it is built

`Node.js` · `Fastify` · `PostgreSQL (Neon)` · `Cloudflare` · `GitHub Actions` · `Playwright` end-to-end tests

Multi-organisation from the start: every NGO gets its own portal, branding and user roles, with approvals for sensitive changes and a full activity record.
